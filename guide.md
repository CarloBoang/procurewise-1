## Key Workflow Summary

1. **Step 1: PPMP Upload / Selection**: The End-User uploads their own PPMP file (PDF/Excel) or selects a registered PPMP, specifying the project title, department, expenditure object, FY, and budget ceiling.
2. **Step 2: PR Creation**: Formulates the PR line items bound by the PPMP budget ceiling, with PhilGEPS catalog lookup, Appendix 60 canvas, and Section 5.1.1 anti-splitting verification.
3. **Procedures 5.1 to 5.15**: Verified by Procurement Officer II $\rightarrow$ logged into PMR & Tracking Slip by Staff $\rightarrow$ BAC RFQ & PhilGEPS Posting by Officer I $\rightarrow$ Abstract of Quotations & MEARB Best-Value evaluation $\rightarrow$ Notice of Award $\rightarrow$ Budget Obligation (ALOBS/BURS) $\rightarrow$ PO contract signing & delivery inspection (IAR).
